Projects

Throughout my academic and professional journey, I have had the opportunity to work on a variety of innovative projects that have allowed me to apply and expand my technical skills. These projects span different domains and technologies, each presenting unique challenges and learning experiences. These include Surrealist, Transago, Starlane Studios, Svantle, Smash Wizards, and Rubicon.

Surrealist

I am actively working with the SurrealDB team on Surrealist, the ultimate IDE for interacting with SurrealDB.

Transago

Transago is a bill tracking app built on top of SurrealDB that I created for my family to use, allowing us to track our bills and expenses. I plan to release and open source this project soon.

Starlane Studios

Starlane Studios is a project that I co-founded with a group of my friends which works on creating a variety of projects, including games.

Svantle

Svantle is a small brand that I have created to house various projects that I plan to release in the somewhat near future including Transago that I listed above, as well as various in development projects like Chronosity (TBA).

Smash Wizards

Smash Wizards was a enterprise-grade Minecraft server network that was created by my friends and I. Alongside my development work on Smash Wizards, I helped to grow our following to over 200,000 people.

Rubicon Platform

I worked as an infrastructure engineer on Rubicon, a Starlane Studios (formerly known as Exodius Studios) project, was a enterprise-grade Minecraft server management platform designed for professional Minecraft servers and events.
